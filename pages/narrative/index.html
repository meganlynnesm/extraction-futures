Two archives, two extractions

For ten thousand years Irish bogs have stored a record of climate, pollution and people. For two hundred years that record was surveyed, drained, milled and burned. Today a new archive, the data centre, stores the data behind everyday life, and draws on the grid, and some of it is being planned on the same cutaway ground.
