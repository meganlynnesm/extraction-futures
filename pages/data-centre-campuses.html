What is drawn

Only building footprints: the halls themselves, not car parks, substations, fences or grounds. Each campus groups buildings run by the same operator, or sharing one business park, within a few hundred metres of each other.

In the Matrix every campus shares one scale, so their sizes compare directly. In the Atlas the island and the buildings are at the same, true scale: zoom in until the halls appear. The faint grid is the Irish Transverse Mercator grid (EPSG:2157).

Dashed outlines are buildings mapped as under construction.

Data

Footprints: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Every way on the island tagged building=data_center or telecom=data_center, plus the buildings inside sites carrying those tags. Retrieved 10 Nov 2026 through the Overpass API.

Coastline: Natural Earth 1:10m, public domain, projected to ITM.

Unattributed means the building is tagged as a data centre in OpenStreetMap but has no name or operator. Names and operators come from OSM tags and were not independently verified.
